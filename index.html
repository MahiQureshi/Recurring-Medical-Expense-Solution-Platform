HOW IT WORKS

From Medical Request To Continuous Support

A simple and transparent process designed for recurring healthcare assistance.

1

Submit Medical Request

The patient enters medical requirements, monthly expenses and treatment duration.

2

Upload Documents

Prescription, medical certificates and previous bills are submitted.

3

Hospital Verification

The hospital verifies whether the patient's medical requirement is genuine.

4

Smart Support Matching

The system identifies NGOs, hospitals and pharmacies suitable for the patient.

5

Receive Support

The patient receives financial assistance, discounts or medical concessions.
